Waqt Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Waqt has no account and no server of its own. Nothing you do in the app is collected, sold or shared. There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

Your location

If you allow it, your location is used on your phone to work out prayer times and the qibla direction. It is never sent anywhere. You can pick a city instead and leave location off.

Data kept on your phone

Your prayer log, settings, bookmarks and saved surahs are stored on your phone. They are included in your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google (Android) backup if you have backups turned on. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

The whole Quran ships with Waqt and reads offline. Waqt fetches two things: extra results for words you search in the Quran, from api.alquran.cloud, and recitation audio when you listen or download, from cdn.islamic.network. Like any web request, these carry your IP address and nothing else about you.

Purchases

Waqt Plus is bought and managed through the App Store or Google Play. Waqt never sees your payment details.

Reminders

Prayer reminders are scheduled on your phone. No push service is involved.

Children

Waqt collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
