Prayer times and qibla

Prayer times and the qibla direction are calculated on your phone from the location and calculation method you choose. They are a guide, not a religious ruling. Methods differ and phones can be inaccurate, so check the times against your local masjid and follow it where they differ.

Quran text

The Quran text is the Uthmani script from Tanzil, with the Pickthall translation, reproduced unchanged. Verify any passage against a printed mushaf before relying on it.

Waqt Plus

Waqt Plus is an optional subscription sold through the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). It renews automatically until you cancel, which you can do at any time in your store's subscription settings. A free trial, where offered, becomes a paid subscription unless cancelled before it ends. Prayer times, the adhan, reminders and the qibla are free.

Your data

Waqt has no account and no server. Your prayer log, settings and saved surahs are stored on your phone and included in your own iCloud or Google backup if you have it on. Deleting the app deletes them.
